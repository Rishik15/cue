# Code of conduct

Cue is a small project and I want it to stay a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and send changes.

## What to expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize the work, not the person.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project, to Rust or to open source.
- Keep discussions on topic and respect that this is maintained in spare time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after a clear no

## Enforcement

Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules may be edited, locked or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the repository. To report a problem with someone's behavior, write to the maintainer through the contact on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Rishik15). Reports are handled privately.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
